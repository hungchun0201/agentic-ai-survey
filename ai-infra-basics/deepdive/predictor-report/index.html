1. Part 1 — the predictor

Half one predicts one served engine step. The classic roofline needs two numbers a GPU rarely sustains. The intended contribution replaces the unexplained multiplier with a product of concrete factors. The audit checks how much is data-backed.

The per-step model (what predictor.py actually computes)

Tstep

t_step = max(T_blocks, N_kern·t_launch) + α(N,T_new)·S(N) + t_samp
▼

Tblocks

N_L·(t_proj + t_attn) + t_light, N_L=32. Each op a roofline max.

Nkern·tlaunch

N_kern = 12·N_L + 10 = 394; dispatch floor.

α·S(N) + tsamp

Host-leak S(N); α=1 eager (line 196).

Droop is real, n=5, with CIs: H100 30.3% (±8.3), H200 26.5% (±10.2), A100-80 20.2% (±20.4), L40S 43.5% (±33.3). Honest range 20.2–43.5%. Causal: clock-lock elasticity 0.98–0.99. The cap sets the clock.

But '→ slower GEMM' is FALSE on datacenter parts. Throughput decline: H100 0.2%, H200 0.1%, A100-80 0.0% (plateau), L40S 17.6% only. η_noclk rising masks droop into a plateau. The honest claim is 'misses 989 and we price that', not 'GEMM gets slower'.

Explicit-clock reconstruction cuts error 29.0%→9.6% overall, 36.7%→3.5% for M≥2048. Caveat: the 3.5% is A100-dominated; H100 residuals stay −14.3% to 26.3% (closure_mape 15.6%).

Blackwell correction (NOT DONE). The '~55% Blackwell droop' is misattributed: RESULTS_LEDGER.json derives 55.8% from the RTX-5090 boost (3090 MHz), not the RTX PRO 6000. The only Blackwell DVFS file is n=1. The n=5 sweep is pending.
Close the Blackwell gap. Rerun bench_gemm_clocktel.py --repeats 5 on a 300 W Max-Q Blackwell + pinned-clock control. Expected: droop 45–55%, decline 5–15% — a second confirmed tight-cap point.

P1.b — Not all 132 SM per wave (cluster co-residence) SOLID (HTML) PARTIAL (paper)

IssueNot all 132 SM per wave. True? Why can't all 132 stay on? Idle SM wasteful?

True: H100/H200 launch grid=112 → η_smused=112/132=0.848. Mechanism corrected: it is thread-block-cluster co-residence (launch-time cap, cudaOccupancyMaxActiveClusters = 7×16 = 112), not a runtime GPC barrier. ~15% of the array dark on Hopper.

Gap: η_wave from a cuBLAS dispatch log; served-kernel transfer only ~72% grid accuracy. Paper shows only the small-M last-wave case, not the 112-cap mechanism. Close: a closed-form per-arch tile selector >90% match, or document the dispatch log as input.

P1.c — Two more factors: TC duty + cluster sync — why TWO? PARTIAL

IssueWhy TWO factors? Is 'fraction TC active' not enough?

Premise corrected: the two are η_active (time-domain occupancy) and η_duty (pipe-domain issue rate). They differ within one SKU; collapsing them hides which confound. η_duty has a 1-param physical form 1/(1+0.31/tiles-per-CTA).

CRITICAL (PARTIAL): The shipped paper does not carry η_active/η_duty. 3_method_gemm.tex has TC_util (a different object) and one measured η_bar that absorbs act·duty·clock — the very lumping the project claims to avoid.

P1.mem — Memory correction: L2? Quantifiable PURELY from params? INSUFFICIENT

IssueIs L2 why peak BW needs a <1 factor? Quantifiable PURELY from params?

The blueprint's mental model (L2 absorbs reuse) is the WRONG mechanism, refuted by our own data; the pure-parameter quantification is genuinely OPEN.

  • Production branch is a per-SKU fitted scalar (h100 0.755, h200 0.669, a100-80 0.75, l40s 0.884). a100-80's 0.75 contradicts its own ncu (0.509). The size-law is NOT wired in.
  • The size-law is OLS, not L2-derived, and regresses the step: per-op MAPE 5.25→2.83 but step decode 12.5→14.17, because per-op errors cancel in the step sum. Fit on 1 SKU.
  • Mechanistic L2 (E1) fails out-of-sample: in-sample r²=0.992 but out-of-sample rel_err gemm 481%, rmsnorm 386%; cliff_collapse_reproduced: false.
  • Our own L2 paper proves reuse is NOT the mechanism: recoverable L2-reuse ceiling < 0.65% of step. So eta_mem<1 is DRAM-streaming inefficiency, not L2 reuse.
Close it (E1, designed). Sweep (op,M,K,N) straddling L2 capacity on 4 SKUs; plot eta_mem vs W/L2_capacity; predict held-out t_mem to ≤8% with no per-op fit + confirm L2 hit-rate≈0. Then re-plug and re-run gemm_full_fix to test the per-op→step cancellation.
